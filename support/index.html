Recording doesn't work

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed in Settings > Memoo. Your first recording may take a moment while the on-device speech model downloads.

Which languages does Memoo understand?

Korean and English. Set Spoken language to Auto and Memoo tells the two apart for you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions and cancel Memoo Plus.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app, open Settings > Restore Purchase.
